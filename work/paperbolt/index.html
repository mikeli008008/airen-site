Before you call

Is Paperbolt a real agency?

No. Paperbolt is a fictional studio Airen built to show what a marketing agency's site can look like. The film and images are AI rendered.

What does a project start with?

A discovery call. We talk about who you sell to and what you want more of, then send a plan.

Do you only work with big brands?

The process is the same for a single shop or a national launch. Only the number of channels changes.

How do I know it is working?

Every campaign has one number we agree on up front, reported to you weekly.
