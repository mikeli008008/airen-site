Good to know

Can I order ahead?

Yes. Pick your drink and a pickup time, and it will be waiting at the counter.

Do you have dairy free milk?

Oat is our default for matcha. Almond and soy are always on hand at no extra charge.

Is the matcha sweetened?

Not unless you ask. The strawberry cloud gets its sweetness from the fruit.

Can I buy beans to take home?

Yes, and we grind them for your brewer while you wait.
