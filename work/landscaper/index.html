Cut through the patio and the bed

Everything you walk on sits on layers you never see. They decide whether a patio stays level after its first winter.

The pergola and the plants come off first

Plants lift with their root balls. They go back into loosened, amended soil, never straight into the clay below.

Stone, then the bedding sand under it

Natural stone sits on a thin bed of sand, so each piece can be set level by hand.

Gravel base on landscape fabric

The compacted gravel carries the weight and lets water drain away. The fabric keeps it from sinking into the subsoil.
