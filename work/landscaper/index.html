Questions

What people ask before the yard walk

I don't really know what I want. Is that a problem?
No. Most people start with a feeling, not a plan. The yard walk and the drawn plan exist to turn "somewhere to sit in the evening" into stone, plants and light you can see before you commit.
Who will be on site during the build?
The project lead who walked your yard runs the build. You get their number and a written schedule before work starts.
Will the plants survive our winters?
We plant natives and hardy grasses chosen for Ontario, and come back after the first season to check how everything settled in.
Can you work around the trees we already have?
Yes. Mature trees are the hardest thing to replace, so the plan is drawn around them and their roots.
Do you take on small yards?
Yes. A small yard needs a plan more than a big one, because every square metre has to work.
Can we build the plan in stages?
Yes. The plan shows the whole garden, so a patio this year and planting next spring still end up as one design.
