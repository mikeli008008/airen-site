Before you come in常见问题

How do I book?

Book online through our booking page, or call or WhatsApp 437 869 5188.

What is the Maple Season Wash Pass?

$79 per month on a two month plan, with wash access up to once per day and member perks.

Can I wash my car myself?

Yes. From 6 to 9 PM every day the bay is X-Wash self service.

Do you remove pet hair?

Yes, as an add on. So are oil film, bug and brake dust removal, clay bar, child seat cleaning, wax and dash cams. Ask for a price when you book.
