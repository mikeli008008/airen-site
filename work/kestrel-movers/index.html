How a move works

Four steps, no surprises

You talk to one person from the first call to the last box.

  1. Tell us about the move

    Rooms, dates, both addresses and anything heavy or fragile.

  2. Get a written quote

    The crew size, the truck, and any packing or storage, all written down.

  3. Moving day

    We pad, load, drive and unload into the rooms you choose.

  4. Settle in

    Furniture goes back together and the empty boxes leave with us.

Questions

Good to know before you book

Do you move outside Toronto?

Yes. We move homes across the region and plan longer moves case by case.

How is the price set?

From what you tell us: the size of the home, access at both ends, and any packing or storage. You get it in writing before moving day.

Can you store things between closing dates?

Yes. We keep your things on racks in our own warehouse until the new place is ready.

Do you move pianos?

Yes. Pianos and other heavy pieces move on dedicated dollies with a crew that does it often.

What should I do before moving day?

Book your building's elevator if there is one, set aside valuables and documents, and tell us about stairs or tight turns.
