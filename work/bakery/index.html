    What happens after 8 p.m.

    2:00 a.m.Four things in the bowl

    Flour, water, salt and our starter. Nothing else goes in, so the flavour has to come from time.

    2:30 to 4:30 a.m.Folded by hand, nine times

    Every half hour the dough is stretched and folded over itself. Each fold traps more air and lines up the strands. It is where our name comes from.

    4:45 a.m.Shaped and scored

    Pulled tight into rounds and batards, then cut with one blade so each loaf opens along its own ear.

    5:30 a.m.On stone, with steam

    The deck oven sets the bubbles and turns the crust thin and crackling. Then the loaves cool on the racks.

    7:00 a.m.Your bag, on the shelf

    Pre orders sit on their own shelf, bagged and named. Walk in, pick up, still warm.

    Good bread takes a whole night. Pre order and you only wait for the door to open.

    Before you order

    What if it sells out before I get there?

    That is what pre orders are for. Anything ordered by 8 p.m. is baked that night and set aside under your name until closing.

    Is your sourdough very sour?

    Mildly. A long cool ferment gives a rounded flavour rather than a sharp one. The seeded rye is the deepest; the country loaf is the gentlest.

    Why pay more than the grocery store?

    Each loaf takes a full night of work by hand, with four ingredients and no shortcuts. Scroll back up to watch the night it goes through.

    When do I pay?

    At the counter when you pick up. No card details online.

    Can I order for an office or a party?

    Yes. Choose “Tray for a group” in the form and tell us the day and headcount. We reply by the next evening.

    Do you deliver?

    Not yet. Everything is picked up at the shop, so the bread reaches you as fresh as it left the rack.
