Good to know

What is toum?

A whipped Lebanese garlic sauce, fluffy and white. It goes on almost everything here.

Is the meat halal?

For a real restaurant this answer comes from the owner and their supplier. It is left open in this demo.

Do you have vegan options?

The falafel wrap with tahini, pickles and turnip has no animal products.

How hot is Habibi hot?

Hot enough that we hand you a mint lemonade with it. Turn the dial down if you are unsure.
