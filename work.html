Larkhollow Landscape Studio

Landscaping. Landscape design and build in west Toronto, redrawn as a field guide folio. Plate I is an engraved block diagram of the garden that draws itself in ink; scrolling cuts it open and lifts the pergola, plants, stone, sand, gravel and fabric apart with a numbered key in real build order, then builds it back. A specimen index of Ontario natives filters by light and season, and the booking form is a yard walk request slip.
Concept

Ninefold Bakehouse

Bakery. Fictional sourdough bakery in Toronto's east end. The first screen is a working pre order composer set as a bakery counter: the headline sits behind a large drawn bag and the order controls run along a receipt strip under it; pick a day and loaves and tomorrow's bag is drawn with them in it. Then the night: a dough sheet is folded nine times by scroll or by hand, shaped, scored and baked. Every loaf is grown in code from its own seed; tap one on the rack to slice it and see its crumb.
