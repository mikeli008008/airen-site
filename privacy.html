Privacy policy

We collect as little as we can, use it only to build and run your site, and never sell it.

What we collect

  • What you send in the free preview request: your name, business name, email and anything you add in the message.
  • What you send in the intake form after purchase: business details, photos, logo and links.
  • Payment details, which go directly to Stripe. We never see or store your full card number.

How we use it

To build your preview and your site, to contact you about your order and your care plan, and to keep records required for tax and accounting.

Who we share it with

Only the services we need to deliver the work: Stripe for payments, our hosting and domain providers for your site, and Google for your Business Profile and our own records. We do not sell or rent personal information.

Cookies

This site does not use advertising or tracking cookies.

How long we keep it

Preview requests that do not become clients are deleted after 12 months. Client records are kept for as long as you are a client and then as long as tax law requires.

Your rights

You can ask for a copy of your information, a correction, or deletion at any time by emailing airen.sites@gmail.com. We handle personal information in line with Canada's PIPEDA.
