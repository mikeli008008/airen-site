Questions

What owners ask before they say yes

Anything else, ask in the preview request. A person replies within one business day.

Do you work with my kind of business?

If you serve local customers, yes: trades and renovators, restaurants and home kitchens, clinics, salons, studios, shops, tutors, agents and professional offices. The package is the same for everyone; the design and the video are built around your business.

What is in the free preview?

A real homepage built with your name, photos and services at a private link, plus a rough cut of your video. If you do not go ahead, nothing is owed and the preview comes down.

Why is this so much less than an agency?

Airen is a one person studio that runs on its own automation. The build, the video edit and the monthly updates are produced by AI tools and checked by a person, so there is no team to pay for.

What happens after I pay?

You fill an 8 minute intake form. The five business days start when it is complete, with two rounds of revisions included.

Do I own the website?

Yes. On full payment the site content and design are yours. If you leave the care plan we send your files within 10 business days.

What counts as an edit on the care plan?

Any small change to your existing pages: text, photos, hours, prices, menu or service items, team members, a promo banner. Send as many as you like by email, WhatsApp or WeChat; we work on one at a time and each goes live within 2 business days, usually sooner. New pages, new sections and redesigns are quoted separately or added for $149 a page.

Can I cancel the care plan?

Any time, from your billing portal. Service runs to the end of the paid month and you keep the site.

Can the site be in another language?

Yes, at no extra cost: English, Chinese or another major language, or two of them side by side. Copy is written for your local customers, not just translated word for word.

I already have a website. Is this for me?

Send the link. We check it on mobile and for speed and tell you the two or three gaps that matter. If the fix is small we will say so.
